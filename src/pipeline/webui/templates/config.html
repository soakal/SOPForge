Narration transcription

On by default. When a capture session's optional tray "Record narration (mic)" toggle produced a narration.wav, and no transcript was uploaded by hand, enabling this runs it through a local speech-to-text model (faster-whisper) before generation — nothing leaves your network. A missing model or unavailable hardware just skips transcription for that session; the document still generates from the steps alone.

Saved to your per-user models.toml.
